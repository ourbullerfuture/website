About these comments

Every comment on this page was written by someone who signed the open letter and agreed to it being published. We show only the details each person chose to share. Nobody's email address is published, and the signature list itself is not on this website. See how your details will be used.

We may shorten a comment for length or fix an obvious typo, and we don't publish anything that names or targets an individual.
